# Bug Report: BUG-005

**Title:** Product images are mismatched when logged in as problem_user

**Severity:** High
**Priority:** High

**Environment:**
- URL: https://www.saucedemo.com
- Browser: Chrome
- Account used: problem_user

**Steps to Reproduce:**
1. Log in as problem_user / secret_sauce
2. Observe product images on the Products page

**Expected Result:**
Each product displays its correct, corresponding image.

**Actual Result:**
Product images are mismatched/swapped, showing incorrect images for one or more products.

**Frequency:** Always

**Notes:**
Misleads users about what they're purchasing — directly undermines storefront trust. Part of a broader pattern of broken interactions under problem_user — see BUG-004 and BUG-006.
