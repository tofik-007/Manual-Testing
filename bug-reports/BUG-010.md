# Bug Report: BUG-010

**Title:** Footer "Terms of Service" and "Privacy Policy" links are not clickable

**Severity:** Low
**Priority:** Low

**Environment:**
- URL: https://www.saucedemo.com
- Browser: Chrome
- Account used: standard_user (confirm across other accounts)

**Steps to Reproduce:**
1. Log in with any account
2. Scroll to the bottom of the Products page
3. Click "Terms of Service" or "Privacy Policy" in the footer

**Expected Result:**
Clicking navigates to the respective policy page (or opens in a new tab).

**Actual Result:**
No response when clicked; links are non-functional.

**Frequency:** Always

**Notes:**
Low severity/priority since this is a static demo site with no real legal content behind these links, but in a production app this would be a compliance-relevant defect (legally required disclosures must be accessible), not purely cosmetic.
