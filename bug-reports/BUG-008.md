# Bug Report: BUG-008

**Title:** Sauce Labs Backpack product image is swapped with an unrelated image when logged in as visual_user

**Severity:** Medium
**Priority:** Medium

**Environment:**
- URL: https://www.saucedemo.com
- Browser: Chrome
- Account used: visual_user

**Steps to Reproduce:**
1. Log in as visual_user / secret_sauce
2. Observe the product image for "Sauce Labs Backpack" on the Products page
3. Compare against other product images on the same page

**Expected Result:**
Sauce Labs Backpack displays its correct product image, consistent with all other items.

**Actual Result:**
Sauce Labs Backpack displays an unrelated image (a dog) instead of the product photo; all other product images render correctly.

**Frequency:** Always, confirmed on repeat login.

**Notes:**
Related to BUG-005 (problem_user image mismatch) — visual_user appears purpose-built by Sauce Labs to isolate one deliberately-broken visual element, useful for demonstrating visual regression testing tools (e.g., Percy, Applitools).
