# Bug Report: BUG-004

**Title:** "Add to Cart" button does not add items when logged in as problem_user

**Severity:** High
**Priority:** High

**Environment:**
- URL: https://www.saucedemo.com
- Browser: Chrome
- Account used: problem_user

**Steps to Reproduce:**
1. Log in as problem_user / secret_sauce
2. Click "Add to cart" on any product
3. Observe cart badge and cart contents

**Expected Result:**
Item is added to cart; cart badge increments; button changes to "Remove."

**Actual Result:**
Item is not added; cart badge does not increment; button does not change state.

**Frequency:** Always, across multiple products tested.

**Notes:**
This blocks the entire purchase flow for this account. Part of a broader pattern of broken interactions under problem_user — see BUG-005 and BUG-006. Recommend investigating a shared root cause rather than treating these as independent defects.
